# Sources catalogue for the daily position search

Each line is: **Name**: `domain` (for WebSearch `allowed_domains`). Notes follow where useful.
The daily search must touch **every group** below on every run (see `SEARCH_INSTRUCTIONS.md`).
Within a group, cover the ★ sources daily. Rotate through the other sources so each one is searched at
least once a week. Use the weekday list at the bottom.

---

## 1. Pan-European job boards and networks
- ★ **EURAXESS** (jobs + MSCA hosting offers): `euraxess.ec.europa.eu`
- ★ **ELLIS jobs**: `ellis.eu`
- ★ **AcademicPositions**: `academicpositions.com`
- ★ **Nature Careers**: `nature.com`. Search "naturecareers postdoc machine learning".
- **Times Higher Education Unijobs**: `timeshighereducation.com`
- **EuroScienceJobs**: `eurosciencejobs.com`
- **ScholarshipDB** (postdoc listings): `scholarshipdb.net`
- **jobRxiv**: `jobrxiv.org`
- **Science Careers (AAAS)**: `jobs.sciencecareers.org`
- **Informatics Europe job platform**: `informatics-europe.org`
- **ApplyKite**: `applykite.com`
- **ResearchGate jobs**: `researchgate.net`
- **LinkedIn** (public job pages): `linkedin.com`. Search "postdoctoral researcher LLM".
- **CLAIRE / ADRA / TAILOR** AI network news and vacancies: `claire-ai.org`, `adr-association.eu`, `tailor-network.eu`

## 2. Field-specific mailing lists and boards (data management, NLP, ML)

## Weekday rotation for the non-★ sources
Search the ★ sources every day. Then, depending on the Europe/Rome weekday, also cover:
- **Monday**: group 1 (non-★) + group 3 fellowships
- **Tuesday**: groups 4 and 5 (Italy extras, Germany)
- **Wednesday**: groups 6 and 7 (France, Netherlands/Belgium)
- **Thursday**: groups 8 and 9 (Switzerland/Austria, Nordics)
- **Friday**: groups 10 and 11 (Spain/Portugal, Central & Eastern Europe)
- **Saturday**: groups 12 and 13 (UK and Ireland extras)
- **Sunday**: group 2 (mailing lists) + anything that returned errors during the week
